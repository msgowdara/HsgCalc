Chapter 1 · Background & Policy Genesis+

India's MSME export sector faced a structural cost-of-credit disadvantage vs global competitors. To bridge this gap, Government of India launched the Export Promotion Mission (EPM) in January 2026 as part of its 'Viksit Bharat' export strategy.

What is Niryat Prothsahan?

'Niryat Prothsahan' = 'Export Encouragement' in Hindi. It is an interest subvention (interest subsidy) scheme that reduces the effective borrowing cost for eligible MSME exporters. The bank extends the benefit upfront and then claims reimbursement from RBI monthly.

Chapter 2 · Eligibility — Who Qualifies?+

Eligible Exporters

  • Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) — as per Udyam Registration
  • Both manufacturer-exporters and merchant exporters (trading exporters)
  • Exports must fall under DGFT notified positive list of 6-digit HSN tariff lines
  • Valid, active IEC (Import Export Code) — not suspended, cancelled, or in Denied Entity List
  • Valid Udyam Registration Number
  • Valid UIN (Unique Identification Number) generated from DGFT portal BEFORE applying for export credit

Ineligibility Conditions

❌ Ineligibility — Six Conditions
  1. Deemed Exports (Chapter 7, FTP 2023) — branch must obtain undertaking confirming NOT covered
  2. Export credit account turns NPA before completion of eligible export cycle
  3. Exports under HSN lines NOT in DGFT positive list
  4. UIN not linked to IEC bank account
  5. Export credit sanctioned before 02.01.2026
  6. No valid Udyam Registration

MSME Graduation Benefit

Exporters who cross the MSME threshold due to increase in investment/turnover during the FY continue to remain eligible for 3 years from the date of reclassification (per MoMSME Notification S.O. 4926(E) dated 18.10.2022), subject to all other conditions.

💡 Plain English
Think of the 3-year grace period as an "MSME exit ramp" — your business can grow without suddenly losing the export subsidy umbrella for the next 3 years.

Multi-Bank Cap — Critical Point

When an exporter avails credit from more than one lending institution:

  • Responsibility to ensure aggregate subvention ≤ ₹50 Lakh per FY rests solely with the beneficiary exporter
  • Any excess claim is recoverable from the exporter as per applicable provisions
  • Bank must obtain a suitable undertaking from the exporter
Chapter 4 · UIN — The Critical Operational Element+

What is UIN?

A Unique Identification Number generated by DGFT when the eligible MSME exporter files an online declaration of intent to avail interest subvention. Must be obtained BEFORE applying for export credit.

UIN Process Flow

Record Keeping — Mandatory

🗂 Branch Must Maintain
Separate UIN-WISE records of ALL claims submitted under the scheme — for the purpose of audit by RBI or any competent authority. Repeatedly emphasised in circulars 090 and 395.

Early Foreclosure Rule

If borrower repays entire loan amount BEFORE stipulated tenure → Branch must report early foreclosure to RBI. Bank stops computing subvention from that point.

Chapter 7 · Exporter Undertaking & Branch Obligations+

The branch must obtain and retain a signed undertaking from the exporter on their letterhead before extending the subvention. Key declarations required:

  • Valid Udyam Registration (copy enclosed)
  • Exports under notified positive list of HSN 6-digit tariff lines
  • NOT covered under Deemed Exports (FTP 2023, Chapter 7)
  • Valid, active IEC — not in Denied Entity List
  • Valid UIN linked to IEC bank account
  • Account is STANDARD on date of claim
  • Aware of ₹50 Lakh annual cap — aggregate across all banks within cap
  • Responsible for excess claims and liable for recovery
  • Will reimburse / authorise BoB to recover subvention if claim rejected by DGFT
📋 Telecom Products Special Declaration
Exporters of telecom products must additionally declare eligibility per Department of Telecommunications notification in the undertaking.

Chapter 9 · Scheme Governance & Special Features+

Governance Features

  • Pilot basis — Government will assess before mainstreaming
  • Subvention benefit is upfront to exporter — bank passes on first, then claims from RBI
  • Rate reviewed bi-annually — preferably last week of March and September
  • Additional support for MSMEs exporting to new geographies — detailed guidelines to follow from DGFT/RBI
  • Reimbursement to banks is monthly and limited to actual subvention extended

🚨 Common Exam Traps

TRAP 1: "Subvention from date of UIN generation" → WRONG. From date of DISBURSAL.

TRAP 2: "UIN can be transferred if bank changes" → WRONG. Zero portability. New UIN mandatory.

TRAP 3: "2.75% on PCFC / forex credit" → WRONG. Rupee credit ONLY.

TRAP 4: "Partial subvention if account turns NPA" → WRONG. NO subvention at all.

TRAP 5: "₹50L cap per bank" → WRONG. ₹50L cap per EXPORTER across ALL banks.

TRAP 6: "Zone submits directly to RBI" → WRONG in BoB. Zone → CO → RBI.

TRAP 7: "8-digit HS Code allowed" → WRONG. Only 6-digit in INSTA Tool.

TRAP 8: "Reimbursement is quarterly" → WRONG. Monthly reimbursement by RBI.

TRAP 9: "Deemed exports eligible at reduced rate" → WRONG. Completely ineligible.
