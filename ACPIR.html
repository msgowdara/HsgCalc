1
Introduction, Applicability & The Big Picture
▾
Why these Directions exist

Until now, Indian banks followed the IRACP norms (Income Recognition, Asset Classification & Provisioning) built on an incurred-loss model — losses were provided for only after a default event occurred. This delayed loss recognition and understated credit risk.

The 2026 Directions move banking to a forward-looking Expected Credit Loss (ECL) framework, broadly aligned with global standards (IFRS 9 / Ind AS 109). Banks must now estimate future expected losses at every reporting date.

Legal foundation & key dates
  • Issued under Sections 21 and 35A of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949
  • Notification dated 27 April 2026; Reference DOR.STR.REC.No.6/21.06.011/2026-27
  • Comes into force on 1 April 2027
  • The earlier IRACP Directions, 2025 stand repealed on commencement (1 April 2027)
  • 13 amendment directions + 1 repeal direction issued alongside
Applicability — who is covered & who is not

Covered ("Commercial Banks"): Banking companies, corresponding new banks (nationalised banks) and the State Bank of India — as defined under clauses (c), (da) and (nc) of Section 5 of the BR Act, 1949.

EXCLUDED: Small Finance Banks (SFBs) · Payment Banks · Local Area Banks (LABs).

Banks must also follow the prudential guidelines for restructured accounts under the RBI (Commercial Banks – Resolution of Stressed Assets) Directions, 2025.

Borrower-level classification (Para 8.9 & 8.10)
  • One NPA = all NPA: If any one exposure to a borrower is NPA, all exposures to that borrower become NPA. Classification is at the borrower level.
  • Upgrade: An NPA may be upgraded to "standard" only when the entire arrears of interest AND principal are repaid — across all facilities for multi-facility borrowers.
  • Borrower and co-borrower are jointly and severally liable.

  • NPA classification also via day-end process; the NPA date = the calendar date for which the day-end process is run.
  • Illustration: Due date 31-Mar → overdue from 31-Mar → NPA on day-end of 29-Jun (the 91st day).
  • Gross/Net Advances and Gross/Net NPAs computed per Annex 4 format.

The Expected Credit Loss (ECL) Framework
▾
What is ECL?

ECL is an unbiased, probability-weighted estimate of credit losses — neither the worst case nor the best case. Banks evaluate a range of possible outcomes and weight them by probability, incorporating historical experience, current conditions, and forward-looking macroeconomic information.

ECL ≈ PD × LGD × EAD  (present value of the shortfall, discounted at EIR)

PD
Probability of Default
12-month PD for Stage 1; Lifetime PD for Stage 2/3
LGD
Loss Given Default
Loss % of exposure after recovery/collateral
EAD
Exposure at Default
Expected outstanding at time of default
Scope — in-scope financial instruments (Para 17)
  • Loans
  • Debt securities (not measured at FVTPL)
  • Trade receivables
  • Lease receivables
  • Loan commitments (incl. undrawn)
  • Off-balance-sheet credit exposures
  • Financial guarantee contracts
  • Any other financial asset with a contractual right to cash

EXCLUDED: investments in subsidiaries, associates & joint ventures.

Fair valuation on transition (Para 19)
  • On 1 April 2027, banks fair-value their entire loan portfolio.
  • Difference between fair value and carrying amount → adjusted against opening retained earnings, NOT through P&L.
  • If fair value ≈ carrying cost, carrying cost is the best evidence of fair value.
Initial recognition & measurement (Para 20–24)
  • Loans originated on/after 1 Apr 2027: at fair value ± directly-attributable transaction costs; subsequently at amortised cost using EIR.
  • Loans outstanding as on 31 Mar 2027: migrate to EIR regime by 31 Mar 2030.
  • Loan commitments / OBS exposures: date the bank becomes party to the irrevocable commitment = date of initial recognition for ECL.
  • POCI assets: use credit-adjusted EIR; only cumulative changes in lifetime ECL recognised thereafter.
5
The Three-Stage Model & SICR
▾
Stage 1
Performing — no SICR / low credit risk
  • No significant increase in credit risk since origination (or low credit risk).
  • Provision = 12-month ECL.
  • Interest income on gross carrying amount via EIR.
Stage 2
Underperforming — SICR occurred, not impaired
  • Credit risk has significantly increased since origination but asset is not credit-impaired.
  • Provision = Lifetime ECL; new minimum floor 5%.
  • Interest income on gross carrying amount via EIR.
  • Rebuttable presumption: > 30 days past due = SICR. Revolving facilities: continuously over limit/DP for up to 60 days.
Stage 3
Credit-impaired / NPA
  • Asset is credit-impaired at reporting date (includes all NPAs).
  • Provision = Lifetime ECL.
  • Interest income on net carrying amount (gross − loss allowance) via EIR.
  • Non-recognition of income on Stage 3 won't attract auditor qualification.
Determining SICR (Para 26–36)
  • Compare risk of default over expected life at reporting date vs at initial recognition.
  • Use reasonable & supportable forward-looking information available without undue cost/effort.
  • SICR criteria must be documented and applied consistently (e.g. number of rating notches, pricing increase, macro deterioration).
  • Collective assessment allowed for portfolios sharing credit-risk characteristics (instrument type, rating, collateral, maturity, industry, geography, LTV).
  • Backstop: when forward-looking info is unavailable, overdue status may be used.
  • Rebuttal of the 30-DPD presumption needs Board / Board-committee approved policy, documentation, validation & periodic review. Inadequate basis → cease rebuttal & recognise SICR.
  • Stage 2 lifetime PD must reflect the higher risk profile — not be mechanically derived from Stage 1 PD.
SICR-testing exemptions (Para 37–38)

No SICR test and no Stage 1 ECL required for:

  • SLR-eligible investments
  • Direct claims on the Central Government
  • Exposures fully guaranteed by the Central Government
  • Exposures to foreign sovereigns, foreign central banks, MDBs, BIS & IMF attracting 0% risk weight

Measurement of ECL & Lifetime Horizon
▾
Credit loss definition by instrument (Para 40)
  • Loans: PV of contractual cash flows due − PV of cash flows the bank expects to receive.
  • Undrawn commitments: PV of contractual cash flows if drawn − PV of expected receipts if drawn.
  • Guarantee: expected reimbursement payments − amounts the bank expects to recover.
📌 A credit loss arises even if the bank expects full payment but later than the contractual date — because ECL considers the timing of cash flows (time value of money).
General vs Simplified approach (Para 41–45)
  • Simplified approach (Annex 2): lease receivables & trade receivables always measured at lifetime ECL, irrespective of stage.
  • General approach: PD–LGD–EAD for all other instruments. Stage 1 → 12-month PD; Stage 2/3 → lifetime PD reflecting the revised risk.
  • ECL on loan commitments considers the expected drawdown portion (within 12 months for 12-month ECL; over life for lifetime ECL).
Lifetime measurement period (Para 46)
InstrumentMaximum period for lifetime ECL
No undrawn componentMaximum contractual period (incl. extension options) — not longer.
Loan with undrawn commitmentPeriod of exposure to credit risk that wouldn't be mitigated by risk-management actions — may exceed contractual notice period.
Revolving without auto-renewal (WCDL, CC, OD)Maximum contractual period; renewal date only counts if the renewal is a substantive fresh reassessment (documented).
Revolving with auto-renewal (credit cards)Based on the period of credit-risk exposure analysed from historical default/drawdown behaviour.
GuaranteeMaximum contractual period of the present obligation to pay/perform.
Probability-weighted scenarios (Para 47–49)
  • Estimate must be unbiased — not worst-case or best-case.
  • Use multiple scenarios, each linking ECL components to macroeconomic variables.
  • Probability weights based on historical experience + expert judgement, under Board-approved governance.

8
Effective Interest Rate (EIR) & Income Recognition
▾
EIR essentials (Para 50–51)
  • EIR = rate discounting estimated future cash flows over expected life to the gross carrying amount.
  • Determined at initial recognition.
  • Includes: contractual terms (prepayment, extension, call options), transaction costs, fees integral to the rate, premiums/discounts.
  • Excludes: expected credit losses.
  • POCI assets use credit-adjusted EIR.
  • Opening ECL on 1 Apr 2027 may interim-use the contractual rate; full EIR migration by 31 Mar 2030.
Stage 1 & 2 income

Interest recognised on gross carrying amount × EIR (accrual basis).

Stage 3 income

Interest recognised on net carrying amount (gross − loss allowance) × EIR. Not on accrual; only actual cash receipts on NPAs.

Income recognition rules
CategoryMethod
Standard / Stage 1 & 2Accrual; interest on gross carrying amount via EIR
NPA / Stage 3Interest on net carrying amount via EIR; income only on actual receipt
Govt-guaranteed overdue advancesNPA-classification exemption does NOT extend to income recognition — income on cash basis only
POCI treatment (Para 24)
  • Credit-impaired at initial recognition.
  • Expected losses baked into the credit-adjusted EIR at origination/transition.
  • No separate impairment allowance at initial recognition.
  • Only cumulative changes in lifetime ECL recognised thereafter as impairment gain/loss.
9
Governance, Model Risk, Disclosures & Transition
▾
Governance & model-risk management (Chapter V)
  • ECL framework and SICR methodology require Board approval.
  • Rebuttal of the 30-DPD presumption needs Board / Board-committee approval, documentation, validation & periodic review.
  • Broad principles for prudent, robust ECL models; consistent application; maintained documentation.
  • Realistic repayment schedules based on borrower cash flows (Para 12).
  • Loan contracts must specify exact due dates, principal/interest breakup, charges, SMA/NPA illustration & appropriation schema (Para 13).
Enhanced disclosures (related amendment directions)
  • Separate disclosure of Stage 1 and Stage 2 provisions (not netted against advances).
  • Detailed credit-quality tables for investments, loans, guarantees & commitments.
  • Reconciliation of loss allowances; fraud reporting.
  • Non-recognition of income on Stage 3 will not attract auditor qualifications.
